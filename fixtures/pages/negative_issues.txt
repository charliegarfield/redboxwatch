Where Maria Stands — The Issues

Lowering the Cost of Living
Families in our district are being squeezed by rising costs. Maria will fight to
lower prescription drug prices, cap the cost of insulin, and crack down on price
gouging. No family should have to choose between paying rent and filling a
prescription.

Protecting Reproductive Freedom
Maria believes these are decisions that belong to individuals and their doctors,
not politicians. She will vote to restore and protect the right to choose.

Good Jobs and a Strong Economy
Maria will invest in apprenticeships, rebuild our roads and bridges, and bring
manufacturing jobs back to the district. She knows what it takes to meet a
payroll because she ran a small business herself.

Fully Funding Our Schools
Every child deserves a great public school. Maria will fight for the resources
our teachers and students need to succeed.

Join us. Together we can build a district that works for everyone.
